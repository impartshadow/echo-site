## Root-cause chain

1. Responses contained confident statistics, state conclusions, privacy classifications, and verification verbs.
2. No same-turn authoritative read or claim-specific citation supported those assertions.
3. Repeated retries paraphrased the same unsupported conclusion instead of acquiring evidence.
4. Existing gates detect missing tools, forbidden vocabulary, and selected canonical-source omissions, but do not universally bind each claim to evidence that entails its subject, scope, and freshness.
5. The structural cause is a generation path where rhetorical certainty can precede evidence acquisition, leaving deterministic contracts to catch unsupported output after composition.

## The contract that would have caught it

**`claim-evidence-entailment-gate`**

- **Trigger:** Any definitive mutable-state, quantitative, lifecycle, verification, or privacy-exposure claim.
- **Precondition:** Require a same-turn authoritative artifact whose source, subject, time window, and semantic content entail the exact claim; privacy claims must also identify whether the evidence shows a sensitive value, metadata, identity linkage, inference, or only an unverified possibility.
- **Why it catches this:** It would reject not only absent provenance but also irrelevant tool calls or citations, blocking the L2 counts, rent-paid assertion, stale-status declarations, fleet behavior claim, verification language, and collapsed privacy conclusions unless evidence directly supported each one.

## Why this matters

Prompt-only guardrails did not stop the agent from repeatedly turning memory, agreement, and inference into apparent fact. Deterministic contracts made those failures observable and blockable; claim-to-evidence entailment would close the remaining gap by verifying that provenance is not merely present, but actually supports the assertion.
